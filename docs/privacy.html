Hatdraw

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Hatdraw is a free Secret Santa draw. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What we store

Only what you type in: the group's name, budget, date and note; the names people join with; wishlists; and the anonymous messages between a Secret Santa and their giftee. We also store who drew whom.

We do not ask for email addresses, phone numbers or real identities, and we do not use cookies. Your phone keeps a random key in its local storage so it remembers which person you are in a group.

Who can see it

  • Anyone with the group link can see the group's details and the list of names.
  • Only you can see your own match and their wishlist. The organiser cannot see the matches unless they choose to show all pairs to the group at the end.
  • Anonymous messages are visible only to the Secret Santa and giftee they were sent between.

How long we keep it

Every group, with its names, wishlists and messages, is deleted automatically 120 days after it was made. The organiser can delete a group at any time.

Technical data

The site runs on Cloudflare. To stop abuse we count requests per network address for up to one hour; these counters are then deleted. Cloudflare may keep standard server logs under its own policy.

Children

Groups are set up by an adult organiser. Do not put personal details beyond a first name in a group.

Contact

Questions or a request to delete a group: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/secret-santa-draw.
