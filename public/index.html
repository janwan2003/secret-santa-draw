How it works

1

Make the link

Name your group, set a budget and a date. You get one link.

2

Share it in the chat

Everyone taps it and types their name. Add a wishlist if you like. The link preview shows how many have joined.

3

Draw names

When everyone's in, the organiser taps Draw. Each person opens the same link and sees only their own match.

Why a group link?

Most Secret Santa generators make the organiser type in everyone's name, email or phone number and then send each person a separate link. With Hatdraw people add themselves.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no ads, no paid plan.

How does each person see only their own match?

When you join, your phone keeps a private key for you. After the draw, the group link shows you only your own match. Nobody else's phone can see it, including the organiser's.

Someone changed phones or cleared their browser. What now?

After joining, everyone can copy their personal link. Opening it on any device signs them back in. If it's lost, the organiser can make that person a new personal link, even after the draw. Nobody else's match changes.

Can I stop couples drawing each other?

Yes. The organiser can mark any two people as "can't draw each other" before the draw.

Someone in the family doesn't use a phone.

The organiser can add them by name and gets a personal link for them to print or pass on.

What if someone joins late?

The organiser can undo the draw, and everyone draws again with the new person included.

Can we see who had whom at the end?

Yes. On exchange day the organiser can show all the pairs to the group. That's optional.

What do you keep about me?

Only what you type: the group name, people's first names, wishlists and messages. They are deleted automatically 120 days after the group was made. See the privacy policy.
